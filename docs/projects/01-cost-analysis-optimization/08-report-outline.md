> **Prepared by:** [Name], Manager – Cost & Management Accounting

**Single-paragraph executive summary** (the elevator-pitch line from [04-key-insights.md](./04-key-insights.md)):

> We compressed 460 bps of EBITDA over 24 months. Of this, **42% (193 bps) is controllable** by Operations & Finance, **30% (139 bps) is recoverable** through stronger LTC pass-through clauses, and the remaining **128 bps is structural input-cost inflation** that must be priced for. ### Page 5 — Plant-B Deep-Dive

(One chart: Plant-A vs Plant-B metrics. Plus a callout box: "Plant-B is structurally more expensive — and it's not just utilisation.")

Narrative addresses the question every executive will ask: *"should we close Plant-B?"* Answer: **No, not yet.** The new aluminium programme has 18 months of ramp, and at ramp-completion utilisation should reach 78% which would close most of the cost gap. **Re-evaluate at end of FY27.**

## Style Rules

1. **One message per page** (the page title is the message).
2. **Numbers always in ₹ Cr / Lakh** with 1 decimal max.
3. **No jargon without footnote**: "ABC", "MUV", "OEE" all defined inline first time.
4. **Action verbs in recommendations:** "Consolidate", "Reduce", "Drop", "Re-price", "Insource".
5. **Every chart has a "so what?" subtitle** above it (not just a chart title).
6. **No more than 6 colours** in the entire document; consistent with the dashboard.
