## Page 1 — Executive Bridge

> **Subject:** FY24 EBITDA Variance — Diagnostic & Corrective Action
> **Period:** Apr-2023 to Mar-2024
> **Issued:** May 2026
> **Prepared by:** Senior Manager, FP&A
> **Reviewed by:** CFO

**Single-paragraph summary:**

> FY24 EBITDA was ₹1.2 Cr against a plan of ₹4.6 Cr — a variance of ₹3.4 Cr. The dominant driver is **sales-mix shift toward Starter plans (+11 ppt) at the expense of Enterprise (-7 ppt)**, accounting for **₹2.1 Cr (62%) of the variance**. This mix shift is causally tied to a ramp-heavy SDR cohort hired in Q1 FY24 combined with a commission plan that did not weight gross-margin contribution. Cost-side overspend (after re-flex for revenue) was ₹0.56 Cr — within normal noise for a Series-B startup. The recommended FY25 commission redesign and tripwire framework recover an estimated ₹2.35 Cr of EBITDA, supporting an FY25 commitment of **₹6.8 Cr (7.6% margin)**.

**Headline bridge chart** (the waterfall) takes ⅔ of the page.

## Page 2 — Revenue-Side Decomposition

(Three-way decomposition: Volume, Price, Mix.)

**Key callouts:**
- Sales hit 94% of revenue (volume miss only ₹0.31 Cr)
- Pricing held within 0.4% of plan
- Mix shift drove 80% of revenue-side EBITDA variance

**Chart:** plan tier mix (Plan vs Actual stacked bar) + ramp-status × mix heatmap.

## Page 3 — Cost-Side Discipline

(Static vs Flexed variance by function.)

**Key points:**
- Flexed cost-overspend: ₹0.56 Cr
- Two specific decisions explain ₹0.30 Cr of that:
  - 3 unbudgeted Q4 events (₹0.09 Cr) — CRO authority limit too high
  - iOS scope addition (₹0.21 Cr) — no re-baselining
- ₹0.18 Cr of R&D variance reclassified to "Sales-mandated" (the POSH workflow)

**Verdict:** cost discipline is largely intact. **Governance gaps are the issue, not overspend culture.**

## Page 4 — What We Will Do Differently

A 2-column table:

| What we will do | Why |
|---|---|
| Redesign commission plan with tier-multiplier | Aligns rep behaviour with EBITDA contribution |
| Pause SDR hiring through Q2 FY25 | CAC investment outpaces productive capacity |
| Lock FY25 mix targets with mechanism, not aspiration | Plan needs *how*, not just *what* |
| Quarterly commission-plan review with Finance | Catches drift in 90 days, not 12 months |
| Threshold-based capex/scope re-approval (>10%) | Prevents silent scope creep |
| 5-tripwire monthly KPI dashboard | Leading indicators, not lagging |

This page is the **action-table** the Board photographs and emails.

## Page 5 — FY25 Commitment

(Scenario bar chart + 1-page summary table.)

| FY25 scenario | EBITDA ₹ Cr | EBITDA margin | Confidence |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Base (no actions) | 3.5 | 4.1% | High |
| + Mix-recovery from commission | 5.4 | 6.2% | Medium-high |
| **+ Cost-discipline tripwires (committed)** | **6.8** | **7.6%** | **Medium-high** |

**Commitment to Board:** ₹6.8 Cr EBITDA at FY25E revenue of ₹89.5 Cr.

**Sensitivity:**
- Downside (Enterprise win-rate stays at 14%): ₹4.2 Cr
- Upside (Enterprise wins recover to 22% by Q3): ₹7.9 Cr

The downside is the credibility test. We commit to ₹6.8, defend down to ₹4.2 with mitigations, and only target ₹7.9 internally as stretch.

## Style Conventions

- All numbers in ₹ Cr, 1 decimal max
- Variances: F (Favourable) / UF (Unfavourable) flag, never colour alone
- "Plan", "Flexed plan", "Actual" labelled consistently — never "budget" / "estimate" / "forecast" used interchangeably
- Variance signs: positive = favourable (so a negative cost variance is an overspend); document this convention in a footnote on every page
